HUNZA VALLY

Hunza Valley is located in Gilgit-Baltistan, in the north of Pakistan. It is often called Heaven on Earth due to its breathtaking beauty. The valley is surrounded by snow-covered mountains like Rakaposhi. It is famous for its cherry blossoms in spring season. The beautiful Attabad Lake is located in Hunza. Hunza has historic forts like Baltit Fort and Altit Fort. The literacy rate of Hunza is very high. The people of Hunza are very hospitable and long-living. The Hunza River flows through the valley adding to its beauty. Every year thousands of tourists visit Hunza Valley.
